Stunning scenic views from this 79-acre tract that has over 150 feet of elevation change. You can see for miles to the east and south from this hilltop property. Located in south Jack County, this property is mostly wooded with scattered live oaks and cedars. Excellent hunting. Ag Exempt. Electricity at the road. One free flowing oil and gas well with no pump jack. Asking: $9,000 per acre.

From Jacksboro take Hwy 4 for 14 miles and turn west onto Halsell Ranch Road. Go 2.25 miles and property will be on the north side of Halsell Ranch.

From Graford, take Hwy 4 north for 6 miles and turn west onto Halsell Ranch Road. Go 2.25 miles and property will be on the north side of Halsell Ranch.
