Precious 2/3 bedroom, two bath brick veneered home has ALL the updates necessary to make it appear as though it were recently built! Freshly painted interior spotlights the nifty, open kitchen/dining area that also cleverly overlooks the den. Great floor plan maximizes the square footage and showcases the newly installed vinyl planked flooring that offers the look of hardwood with less maintenance! Newly installed French doors enhance the view of the covered patio and nice storage building; these are but two of the features of this exceptionally large, fenced back yard (90’ X 189’). Other nice amenities include the wood burning fireplace, fabulous storage room adjacent to the one car attached garage and window treatments/blinds throughout the home. 1018 West College is a must see.
