Absolutely beautiful 106 acres in northeastern Jack County with FM Road 2127 frontage near Newport, TX, just 1.5 miles off of Highway 59. Property is positioned approximately 21 miles north of Jacksboro, Texas and 14 miles west of Bowie, TX. Thick cover combined with rolling terrain and great soil provide for a property that has so much to offer. This property is turn-key with its 2,100 sq. ft. barn with slab floor as well as a small weekend cabin! The property has a good water well, electricity and a large stock tank. Property comes with a Kubota 35HP Diesel w/ Mower & Box Blade, Kubota ATV Single Bench Diesel, 20’ Double Axle Trailer for Tractor and various tools.
