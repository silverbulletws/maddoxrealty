Bet you didn’t realize one could enjoy living in a log cabin nestled on three large lots within the city limits of Jacksboro? This rustic beauty offers two very large bedrooms and sports an abundance of closets and storage. Rugged and pastoral are the perfect words to describe this cabin, both how it looks and how it feels while viewing. The open floor plan entails a roomy kitchen overlooking the cozy wood-burning fireplace in the spacious den. This home appears invitingly comfortable with its “sit-a-spell” covered patio that beckons hospitality as does the two-car detached garage with electricity. Even when approached from the outside, the sit-a-spell porch beckons with a promise of hospitality. Relaxing in front of a crackling fire, surrounded by warm, honey-hued logs, is what cabin living is all about in this 1,450 square foot dwelling positioned on 1.037 acre. SOLD!
