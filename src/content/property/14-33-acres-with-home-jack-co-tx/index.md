COUNTRY LIVING AT ITS BEST ………. This lovely 2,918 square foot brick home just screams of privacy nestled on 14.33 acres that is beautifully adorned with oaks and mature hardwoods. Built in 1999, the main house sports four bedrooms and two full baths, powder bath and additional bath adjoining the laundry room, formal dining/study, and informal living room. The awesomely open kitchen/breakfast area overlooks the den that features a massively handsome rock fireplace, cathedral ceilings and ornate bookcases. The upstairs room could easily be utilized as either a media room or additional bedroom. Viewing the landscaped back yard from the totally glass engulfed sunroom is truly mesmerizing during the spring……. anxiously waiting those warmer days soon to be here for that relaxing dip in the pool/jetted hot tub! A separate guest house simply has it all…….. Central heat/air, one/two bedrooms, self-contained kitchen and a nifty loft that can accommodate additional guests or house the new pool table! Good water well, aerobic system, two working barns, large, new four stall barn/RV storage are but a few of the extras that will certainly catch your eye……. 3391 FM Road 2190
