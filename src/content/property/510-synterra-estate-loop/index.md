**This charming home nestled on 7.15 acres and within minutes of Jacksboro will quickly allure you to living the simpler life! Watching the turkey graze from your pleasing covered patio will be one of the many simple pleasures you will enjoy. The brick home with a metal roof offers lots of room, including 4 bedrooms and 3.1 baths. There are additional rooms that one can be very creative with, a large kitchen and den that sports a woodburning fireplace. If a home office is a must, no worries…….. this property offers an office with a separate bath and its own exterior entrance conveniently positioned at the front of the home for easy access. But perhaps the single most alluring amenity is the detached metal barn, constructed in 2001. This barn parallels no other offering a ten-foot overhead door, two additional wide doors for easy loading as well as a ramp. Exceptional lighting, including skylights will mean that one rarely uses electricity while working! Lots of entertaining can occur in this hefty fenced back yard area with a separate dog run, in addition to the perimeter being fenced on three sides. Mature trees and a nice stock tank are additional amenities that will enhance your country life.**
