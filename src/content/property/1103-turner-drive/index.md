SHOCKING will be the feeling one gets when entering this charming 1550 sq. ft. home at 1103 Turner Drive and discovering that it isn’t “brand new”! The interior as well as the exterior of this property look new. Freshly painted walls adorn this three-bedroom, two bath home along with beautiful tile flooring in the kitchen, dining room/living room. One will want to linger longer in the freshly updated baths. How many other homes will you find offered at $99,900 that have beautiful birch cabinetry, fenced corner lot (82.4’ X 118’) sporting a nice storage building with a concrete slab, “almost new” covered patio and an oversized two car carport? Definitely a “must see”! Sold!
