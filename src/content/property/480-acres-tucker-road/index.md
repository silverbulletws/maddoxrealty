This sprawling 480-acre tract is just west of Perrin, TX located in far southeast Jack County. There are two creeks on the property that feed into East Fork Kichi Creek. Large neighbors on the north, west and east sides. Abundant wildlife include whitetail, turkey, dove and hogs. The property is mostly covered with large, mature mesquites and cedar elms. Good native grasses. The property has a set of large working pens attached to a trap. This property has over 1.4 miles of county road frontage on Tucker Road. This allows for great upside if the property owner wishes to subdivide in the future. Asking price: $7,950 per acre

Electricity: Electricity is on Turner Road. Tri-County Electric Co-op is the electrical provider.

Water: There are no water wells on the property. There are immediate neighbors to the south with water wells making in the range of 1 to 8 gallons per minute.

Oil and Gas: Surface Only. There is one active oil and gas well on the property. It is located at the far southwest part of the property and has its own entrance. Eagleridge Operating. There are two Colt natural gas, buried pipelines at the southeast corner. Overall, minimal oil and gas activity for a property of this size.
