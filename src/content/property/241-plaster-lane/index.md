7-Acre lake all to your own, situated on 33.59 acres off Hwy 380 just outside Jacksboro, TX. Scattered post oaks with many canopied near the lake. Picturesque views from the back porch that are second to none.

The property is located a few miles outside of Jacksboro on 380 W. Surface only. Ag Exempt.
