Absolutely stunning acreage located in southeastern Jack County less than a mile from Parker County. This pristine tract of land is undisturbed with unlimited potential. Stunning landscape that has large, mature post oaks, deep topsoil and plentiful native grasses and vegetation. Abundant wildlife include whitetail, hogs, dove and turkey. Property has frontage on both FM 2210 and DPS Tower Road.

Landscape: Mostly level from north to south with beautiful draws that supply three stock tanks on the property. Thick sandy loam soil provides for the massive post oaks and pecan trees. Heavy hardwood cover throughout the majority of the property with secluded openings making it ideal for potential home sites or recreational sites.
