A truly rugged and stunning 250 acres located just 6 miles southwest of Graham, TX. A piece of the hill country in Young County. This property has significant elevation change with its two hill ridges and Flatrock Creek dissecting it. The property has two stock tanks as well as a natural spring at the north end running south. It has electricity as well as a Fort Belknap tap at Finis Rd/FM 2179. Flatrock Creek is cut into two veins in the middle of the property with large rock outcrops that add to the uniqueness of the property. Abundant wildlife include whitetail, hogs, dove and turkey. Ag Exempt. Asking price: $6,250 per acre.
