Amazing curb appeal will welcome you when you drive up to 529 North Stewart Street and observe the beautiful, mature Burkett pecan trees and persimmon trees among others, that adorn the front and back yard. The four-bedroom, two bath home offers an array of amenities including a large metal covered patio area overlooking the spacious back yard that is fenced with two separate areas. The one-half+ acre secluded lot (100’ X 225’) offers a perimeter fence as well as a separate back fenced area and an additional dog run/garden area. You’ll be impressed with the roomy, yet bright and cheery kitchen that adjoins the large office/study. The den is adorned with the original hardwood flooring and the remainder of the home offers laminate flooring and tile. SOLD!
