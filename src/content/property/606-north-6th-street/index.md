SHOCKING will be the feeling one gets when entering this charming 1800+ sq. ft. home at 606 North 6<sup>th</sup> and discovering that it isn’t “brand new”! The interior as well as the exterior of this property look “new”. Freshly painted walls adorn this three-bedroom, two bath home along with skillfully restored wood flooring in the breakfast room and bedrooms. In addition to this, the oversized den has fresh, new carpet as well. One will want to linger longer in the recently updated baths. How many other homes will you find offered at $129,000 with the square footage that this one offers combined with a large backyard sporting a covered carport/patio and a very nice storage building with electricity? Definitely a “must see”!
