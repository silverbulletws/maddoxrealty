Absolutely beautiful 50 acres north of Jacksboro roughly 10 miles on Post Oak Rd. The property has electricity running throughout with a 1,500 SF metal barn and two covered parking structures, one having a concrete foundation. Picturesque terrain with mostly large post oaks and great native vegetation. The property has two stock tanks as well as some nice bottom land. Abundant deer, hog, turkey and dove.
