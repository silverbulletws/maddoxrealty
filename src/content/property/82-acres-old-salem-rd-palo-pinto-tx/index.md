82 Acres Fronts Old Salem Road right off of the I-20 access road near the intersection of I-20 and Hwy 281 in the Santo School District. The property has a nice three bedroom, one bath residence as well as 2 stock tanks and a 2 acre lake. The property has great elevation change offering nice views with the residence sitting at one of the highest points. The property is roughly 70% wooded with a mix of Live Oaks, Mesquite and Elms. Good hunting. Some minerals will be conveyed.
