Rich hardwoods cover the majority of this 92-acre tract located just minutes northwest of Jacksboro, TX. The property is mostly post oaks with some scattered mesquites. There is a previously cultivated field on the southeast corner of the property. There is one large stock tank on the property. The property is being divided from a larger tract and will be fully fenced and accessed by its own private road. Surface only. Ag Exempt. Very large ranch in two sides of the property. Excellent hunting. Asking: $7,350 per acre.

Deed restrictions: No subdividing, no manufactured/modular homes, and no trash pits.

From Jacksboro, TX, take Hwy 281 for just over a mile and turn right (north) onto Rogers Rd. Go 3 miles on Rogers Rd and turn left (west) onto Hammon Lane. The property’s entrance will about a quarter of a mile down Hammon Lane on the left (south).
