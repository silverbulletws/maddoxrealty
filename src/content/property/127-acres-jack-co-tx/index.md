Gorgeous 127 acres on FM 2190 just 8 miles north of Jacksboro, TX. This 127-acre property has significant elevation change with scenic vantage point spanning all the way to Jacksboro. The property has great soil and abundant wildlife. The property has electricity, water well, barn with concrete floor and electricity, two stock tanks and working pens in good order as well as nice road through out. The property is roughly 60% wooded with post oaks, live oaks and mesquite and the rest open.
