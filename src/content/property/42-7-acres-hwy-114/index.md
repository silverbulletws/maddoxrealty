Breathtaking views and an incredible homesite opportunity make this 42.7-acre ranch west of Jacksboro truly stand out. The property features an elevated peak with expansive 360-degree views, offering a spectacular setting for a custom dream home and a place to enjoy the beauty and privacy of the North Texas countryside.

The ranch is well-suited for a variety of uses, including a private homesite, hunting and recreation, or a livestock operation. A significant stand of mature hardwoods adds natural beauty to the property while providing valuable cover and habitat for wildlife. For the outdoor enthusiast, the combination of elevation, timber, and diverse wooded acreage creates an attractive recreational setting.

Access is another major advantage. Located just west of Jacksboro and approximately 10 minutes from town, the property offers the peaceful feel of a rural ranch while remaining conveniently accessible. Nearly half a mile of frontage along Highway 114 provides excellent access and visibility, while well-maintained roads throughout the ranch make much of the property easily accessible.

Electricity is available at the highway, and the property is fully fenced, adding to its versatility for future improvements, livestock, or recreational use.

Whether you’re looking to build a secluded country home, establish a small ranch, enjoy hunting and recreation, or simply invest in a property with exceptional views and convenient access, this ranch offers a rare combination of elevation, scenery, accessibility, and privacy.

Properties with a view like this do not come around often in Jack County and it truly sets this ranch apart. From the elevated homesite potential, the panoramic 360-degree views provide the opportunity to create a truly special place to call home.
