Beautiful 330 Acres in Jack County located just 3 miles north of Jacksboro, TX on Hwy 59. The property includes 3 stock tanks, nice elevation change and is mostly wooded with minimal improved pastures. The property has abundant wild game including: whitetail, feral hogs, turkey and a tremendous amount of dove. The property is also suited for cattle with its abundant water and lush natural vegetation. Clean, turn-key property.
