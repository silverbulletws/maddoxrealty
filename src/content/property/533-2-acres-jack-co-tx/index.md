Picturesque 533.28-acre ranch just nine miles north of Jacksboro, TX off of FM Road 2190 on J.T. Graves Road. This pristine, beautiful and sprawling ranch with numerous amenities is a unique opportunity. The ranch offers a small hunter’s cabin, electricity, an additional 8’X12’ trailer and barn with working pens. Abundant food plots, improved coastal pastures, large creek sporting a bridge crossing, good fencing and large government lake are all offerings this property provides.
