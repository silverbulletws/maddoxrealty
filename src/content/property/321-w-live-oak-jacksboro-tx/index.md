Sprawling, spacious and serene will still not adequately describe this magnificent mid-century built brick home located at 321 West Live Oak. The three/four bedroom, 3 ½ bath home offers 3,608 square feet of living area and is well positioned on an unbelievably large lot consisting of 2.653 acres. The original hardwood flooring adorns the spacious dining room and living room, while three fireplaces accent the living room, informal living area and sun room. The original copper hardware still abounds throughout portions of the home displaying the quality and style of its era. And if that is not enough, outdoor offerings include a metal barn (1,080 square feet) with an upstairs that is screaming to be renovated for a man cave/playroom as the original wood rafters/beams are still present. Truly a home that will melt your heart upon viewing…….
