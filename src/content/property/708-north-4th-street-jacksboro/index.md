If “location” and “quality of workmanship” are your priority for residential living, then look no further than 708 North 4<sup>th</sup> Street. This 2000+ square foot brick home was custom built in 1959 and has remarkably had only ***<u>one</u>*** owner! Ranch style three bedroom, two bath home offers more storage than most homes twice its size! Spacious family room and a separate roomy den, along with a particularly nice utility room make this a very unique home. The corner lot offers over a half acre of fenced yard, mature pecan trees, an amazing iris garden, an exceptionally nice brick storage building with a covered sitting area, and so much more.
