This brick veneered 2200+ square foot home is uniquely positioned on 1.416 acres, featuring a fenced back yard and patio area, as well as two separate fenced areas just south and west of the primary back yard. This classic home boasts three large bedrooms and 2.5 baths. The master suite sports two separate baths (one offering a jetted tub). The cozy den with a woodburning fireplace adjoins the kitchen/breakfast area. A formal living room graces this home as well as a precious sunroom offering lots of natural light and spectacular views overlooking the sizable backyard.
