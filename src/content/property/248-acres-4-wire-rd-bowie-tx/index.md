Scenic 248 acres located in southeast Clay County just 12 minutes southwest of Bowie, TX. Half of the minerals go with the property. The property is sloped from north to south with beautiful, unobstructed southern views. Electricity is at 4 Wire Road. The property has two large stock tanks. No water wells on the property, but neighboring properties have water wells in the range from 8-30 gallons per minute.

Terrain: The property is heavily wooded on the north side made up primarily of post oaks. The southern side is mostly open with scattered mesquite and the two stock tanks. The property also has a fenced off cultivated field at its southeast corner. Excellent hunting due to its terrain and large neighbors. The property could be used for hunting, agriculture or to build a home with the elevated views.

Minerals: The owner owns half of the minerals which will be conveyed with the sale. There is no active production on the property.
