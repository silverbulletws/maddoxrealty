Perfect 13.5 acres with home just 16.5 miles due north of Jacksboro, TX near Post Oak. This property has a 3 bed / 2 bath brick home, a barn connected to working pens as well as a storage shed with a concrete floor. The property offers its own water well and septic system. The elevation changes are significant, and coupled with the nice hardwoods on the northern side and the scattered mesquites on the southern side, make this a very unique property.
