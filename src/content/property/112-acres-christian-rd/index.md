Diverse 112 acres located in northwest Jack County surrounded by large neighbors. The property has electricity and a water well. The property has 2 large stock tanks and 2 smaller stock tanks. The majority of the property is scattered hardwoods, mainly post oaks and some cedar elms. The northwest corner of the property has a cultivated field that is fenced off with two stock tanks within the fence. Excellent hunting with abundant whitetail, hogs, turkey and dove. Ag Exempt. Asking price: $7,500 per acre.

Shown by appointment only.

From Jacksboro, TX, take Hwy 281 west for 6 miles and turn north towards Wichita Falls. From there continue on Hwy 281 for 16 miles. Turn west (left) onto Westford Rd and go 300 feet and south (left) onto Christian Road. Go 1 mile and the property will be on the west side of Christian Road.
