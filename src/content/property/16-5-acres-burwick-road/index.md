Discover a rare opportunity just minutes from Jacksboro, Texas. This approximately 16.5-acre tract offers the ideal setting to build your dream home while enjoying the advantages of being outside the city limits, all just 4 miles from downtown Jacksboro.

The property features an attractive blend of mature wooded areas and open pasture, providing both privacy and flexibility for a homesite, recreation, or livestock. Well-maintained interior roads offer excellent access throughout the property. Additional improvements include two stock tanks, quality perimeter fencing, and healthy native grasses suitable for grazing.

Electricity is available along the southern boundary. Neighboring property has a 7.5 gpm water well. The property has no oil and gas activity and is subject to deed restrictions to help protect the area’s value and character.
