1,500 SF Barndominium on 51.16 acres that includes a master bedroom and attached bath with walk-in closet, tiled shower, and double vanity. 10’ foot ceilings. Residence also includes a guest room, guest full bath, open living room, dining room and kitchen. The residence has a rain collection system in place that has three (3) 4,000-gallon storage tank. The barndominium was completed mid-2023.
