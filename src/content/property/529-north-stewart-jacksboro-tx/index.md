Amazing curb appeal will welcome you when you drive up to 529 North Stewart Street and observe the beautiful, mature Burkett pecan trees, persimmon trees and lush gardens that adorn the front and back yard. The four bedroom home offers an array of amenities including a large metal covered patio area that overlooks the spacious back yard that is fenced with two separate areas. The one-half+ acre secluded lot (100’ X 225’) offers a perimeter fence as well as a separate back fenced area and an additional dog run/garden area that is also fenced. You’ll fall in love with the roomy, yet bright and cheery kitchen that adjoins the large office adorned with office furniture that will remain. The amazing original hardwood flooring in the den will stun you, as will the sales price.
