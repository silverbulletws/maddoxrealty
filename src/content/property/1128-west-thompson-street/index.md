**This unique portion of real estate will make for a great investment property. Almost 1.75 acres sporting frontage on West Thompson Street as well as on North Ninth Street; this property offers unlimited possibilities. The land lends itself to the construction of quadruplexes, townhomes and so much more ….. Property offers an already existing older home in need of some repair since the tornado, but consisting of amazingly good bones. There is shiplap and original hardwood flooring throughout most of the 1660+ square foot three bedroom, two bath home. Hardy board siding and new windows were installed in 2016. Property also includes a newly renovated guest house/efficiency with bath. Handsome native stone encompasses the storage building that sports electricity that was formerly the well house. RV hookup (50 amp) is also available on this acreage.**
