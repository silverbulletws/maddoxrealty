The historic Bar MR Ranch is on the market. Part of the larger Williams Ranch, Bar MR Ranch offers 1,615.85-surveyed acres on County Road 15, just 3.7 miles south of Hwy 86, between Turkey and Estelline, TX. The property has Buffalo Creek running almost the entirety of the western portion of the property offering picturesque draws and banks that provide ample cover and food for wildlife. This property has been in the same family for almost 45 years and has been well maintained and managed the entirety of their ownership.

Game Include: Whitetail, Mule Deer, Bob-White Quail, Blue Quail, Dove & Turkey.

Water: The property has one windmill that provides water to the southern pastures of the property. The property has another water trough with flow lines running from the neighbor’s water well to the north.

Electricity easement has been granted from the northern neighbor. Existing electricity, where the electrical easement will be granted, is roughly 2,700 feet away from entrance of Bar MR Ranch

Access: The property’s access is off County Road 15 that connects to Hwy 86 3.7 miles to the north. From its entrance off Hwy 86, County 15 is roughly 10 miles west of Turkey, TX and roughly 20 miles east of Hwy 287/Estelline, TX.

Asking Price: SOLD!
