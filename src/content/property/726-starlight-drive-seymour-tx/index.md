An amazing custom brick veneered home is now on the market that has only previously been owned by two families. Built in 1962, the quality of workmanship displayed throughout this home is guaranteed to impress. Positioned on a corner lot (90’ X 110’), the sprinklered front yard offers a beautiful array of St. Augustine grass and mature pecan trees. The private back yard sports a patio and two extraordinary dog pens. Custom built-in’s in the large split-level den and the roomy office are remarkable. This 2,058 sq. ft. three-bedroom, two bath home could easily be considered as a four bedroom; utilizing the office as an additional bedroom. Sold!
