If you have yearned to own a Jack County estate, your dream can become a reality. This 1 ½ story grandeur is a turn-of-the century home that offers impeccable, timeless style intertwined with an amazing updated kitchen that still holds true to the “old-school” architecture yet uniquely blends with the new quartz counters, stainless appliances and very functional grand island. The home easily accommodates for formal and informal entertaining, including a formal living room large enough to house a grand piano as well a spacious dining area. The separate informal living area is equally as roomy. Downstairs offers three/four bedrooms and four baths, not to mention the nifty bay windows in the breakfast area overlooking the patio situated on a 1+ acre lot. Upstairs sports one enormous room with two baths that is truly stunning. One cannot overstate the amenities that this home has to offer…… 10 and 11 foot ceilings with ornate crown molding, gas fireplaces in informal living area and master suite, water well, detached three stall carport and storm cellar. Look no further than 108 South 7<sup>th</sup> Street for your “Dream Home” that has been amazingly well preserved for your enjoyment
